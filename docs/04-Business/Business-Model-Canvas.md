## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Full canvas in action:** Motorist on Sheikh Zayed Road requests tow → matched to 3 providers (Key Activity) → selects verified operator (Value Prop) → pays AED 280 via app (Revenue: 18% commission) → provider paid in 48 hours (Value Prop to supply) → insurer co-marketing drove app install (Channel: partnership).

**Example 2 — B2B revenue stream:** Fleet operator with 500 vehicles signs annual dispatch contract → flat monthly platform fee + per-job commission → partner portal tracks SLA → reduces fleet's multi-vendor admin cost (Value Prop) → 12% of Year 2 revenue from B2B.

**Example 3 — Subscription upsell:** Top-rated battery specialist in Deira subscribes to Premium Provider tier → priority placement in top-3 offers → completes 25 additional jobs/month → pays AED 499/month subscription (8% revenue stream contribution).

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Two-sided marketplace (no fleet) | Asset-light; scales with provider network |
| Commission-primary monetization | Aligns revenue with successful job completion |
| B2B as secondary revenue, primary demand | Insurer volume reduces B2C CAC |
| Verification as non-delegable activity | Trust is the product in emergencies |
| Launch cost-heavy; Year 2+ margin focus | Classic marketplace density investment |

---

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Chicken-and-egg (supply/demand) | Critical | Zone-gated launch; provider incentives; referral programs |
| Revenue concentration on commission | Medium | Diversify into B2B and subscriptions by Year 2 |
| Key partner dependency (single insurer) | Medium | Multi-partner pipeline; B2C brand building |
| Cost structure exceeds GMV growth | High | Unit economics gates on expansion decisions |

---

## Assumptions

- 18% blended take rate achievable without provider mass churn
- Provider network reaches 150+ verified before consumer marketing scale
- B2B partnerships contribute 25%+ of job volume by Year 3
- Cost structure flips from investment-heavy to margin-positive by Month 22–26
- Marketplace model legally classifiable as intermediary in UAE

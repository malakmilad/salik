**Salik Assist turns roadside emergencies from stressful guesswork into a fast, transparent, and trusted rescue experience—for everyone on the road.**

| Stakeholder | One-Line Value |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Motorists** | Three verified offers. One choice. Tracked arrival. Fair price. |

## Customer (Motorist) Value Proposition

### Problem

When a vehicle breaks down in the UAE, motorists face:
- Unknown wait times on insurer hotlines
- Unverified strangers from informal tow networks
- Surprise pricing with no recourse
- No visibility until help arrives
- Separate calls for different problems (tow vs. battery vs. tire)

### Solution

Salik Assist provides a single app where motorists request help, compare three verified provider offers with clear ETAs and price ranges, track arrival live, pay digitally, and hold providers accountable through ratings and dispute protection.

### Key Benefits

| Benefit | Customer Outcome |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Speed** | Offers in minutes, not 30+ minute hold times |
| **Choice** | Compare 3 providers; pick the best fit |
| **Clarity** | Price range before commitment; no surprise bills |
| **Confidence** | Verified license, insurance, background, and ratings |
| **Convenience** | Tow, battery, tire, fuel, mechanic—one app |
| **Safety** | Share trip status; tracked arrival; platform accountability |
| **Recourse** | Dispute resolution and guarantee fund |

### Customer Value Statement

> *"When my car stops, I open Salik Assist, see three trusted options with clear prices, pick one, watch them arrive, and pay fairly—without fear."*

## Provider Value Proposition

### Problem

Independent roadside professionals struggle with:
- Irregular demand and idle capacity
- Cash payment disputes and slow collections
- No digital reputation beyond word of mouth
- Customer acquisition cost (signage, WhatsApp groups, referrals)
- Competing with informal operators who undercut on price

### Solution

Salik Assist delivers qualified, pre-accepted job leads to verified providers—with digital payment guarantee, transparent commission, reputation building, and full business independence.

### Key Benefits

| Benefit | Provider Outcome |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Demand** | Qualified leads delivered to your phone |
| **Payment** | Guaranteed payout within 48 hours |
| **Reputation** | Digital ratings that grow your business |
| **Independence** | Non-exclusive; your business, your rules |
| **Fair pricing** | You set rates within guardrails; compete on service |
| **Efficiency** | Pre-accepted jobs; no haggling on-site |
| **Growth** | Premium tiers for enhanced visibility |

### Provider Value Statement

> *"Salik Assist fills my schedule with customers who already agreed to my price—and pays me within 48 hours, every time."*

## B2B Partner Value Proposition

### Problem

Insurers, fleets, and rental companies face:
- Policyholder/customer dissatisfaction with roadside wait times
- Manual dispatch across multiple tow vendors
- No real-time SLA visibility
- Inconsistent pricing across emirates and providers
- Legacy call-center models with poor NPS

### Solution

Salik Assist provides a managed marketplace network with SLA reporting, pre-negotiated rate cards, co-branded customer experience, and verified provider quality—without the partner building or maintaining dispatch technology.

### Key Benefits

| Benefit | Partner Outcome |
|---------|----------------|
| **NPS uplift** | Modern, tracked, transparent customer experience |
| **SLA visibility** | Real-time and monthly reporting dashboards |
| **Cost control** | Pre-negotiated rate cards by zone and category |
| **Coverage** | Multi-provider network across emirates |
| **Simplicity** | Single contract vs. dozens of tow vendor agreements |
| **Quality** | Verified providers with ratings and dispute resolution |
| **Speed to market** | Launch in months, not years of internal build |

### B2B Value Statement

> *"Give your customers the roadside experience they expect in 2026—powered by Salik Assist behind your brand."*

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Expat value:** British expat on E311 flat tire, first month in Dubai. Opens Salik Assist, selects English, receives 3 offers in 2 minutes, picks highest-rated provider (4.9 stars), tracks arrival, pays AED 150 by card. Tells colleagues: "This is what you need here."

**Example 2 — Provider value:** Al Quoz tow operator joins Salik Assist. Month 1: 40 jobs, AED 9,200 gross, paid within 48 hours every time. Builds 4.8 rating. Month 3: upgrades to Premium tier; jobs increase 35%. Keeps offline customers too (non-exclusive).

**Example 3 — Insurer value:** Insurer routes 500 policyholder roadside events through Salik in Month 1. Average ETA drops from 52 to 34 minutes. Policyholder roadside NPS: 29 → 51. Insurer renews and expands to second emirate.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Lead value prop with transparency + choice | Validated as top customer pains in research |
| Provider value prop leads with payment guarantee | Top provider recruitment driver |
| B2B value prop leads with NPS, not cost savings | Partners buy experience upgrade first |
| Segment-specific props for expat and women drivers | High willingness to pay; underserved |
| Avoid "cheapest" value framing | Commoditizes; attracts wrong supply |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Value prop promises exceed launch delivery | Critical | Geo-gating; honest marketing; phased rollout |
| Provider value undermined by commission | High | Zero-commission launch; demonstrate ROI |
| B2B value requires features not ready | Medium | LOI-driven roadmap; pilot scope |
| "Uber new" brand confusion weakens trust prop | Medium | Verification badges; partner co-brand |
| Competitor copies top-3 messaging | Medium | Execute and build brand first |

## Assumptions

- Transparency and choice are top-2 customer purchase drivers in emergency context
- Provider payment guarantee converts 60%+ of field sales prospects
- B2B partners value NPS improvement over marginal cost reduction
- Expat segment represents 40%+ of Dubai launch-zone target customers
- Value proposition resonates in both Arabic and English with cultural adaptation

## Cost Structure Overview

Salik Assist follows a **marketplace cost profile**: high variable acquisition costs at launch, declining unit costs at scale, and improving gross margins as network effects compound.

| Cost Type | Year 1 % of OpEx | Year 3 % of OpEx |
|-----------|------------------|------------------|
| Variable (CAC, provider incentives) | 50% | 35% |
| Semi-variable (ops, verification) | 30% | 30% |
| Fixed (G&A, leadership, office) | 20% | 35% |

**Total Year 1 OpEx estimate:** $2.8M–$3.2M (UAE operations, pre-KSA scale)

---

## Variable Costs

Costs that scale directly with job volume, customer count, or provider count:

| Category | Description | Est. Year 1 | Driver |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Customer acquisition (CAC)** | Performance marketing, referrals, app store | $840K | New customer sign-ups |
| **Provider acquisition** | Field sales, referral bonuses, launch incentives | $420K | Verified provider sign-ups |
| **Payment processing** | Card/wallet transaction fees (~2.5%) | $105K | GMV |
| **Promotional credits** | First rescue free, referral credits | $168K | Redemption rate |
| **Provider commission subsidies** | Zero-commission launch period | $126K | Launch zone jobs |
| **Surge bonuses** | Supply-side peak incentives | $84K | Peak demand events |
| **Dispute fund payouts** | Customer guarantee refunds | $42K | Dispute rate (~2%) |

**Variable cost per completed job (Year 1 blended):** ~$8.50

**Variable cost per completed job (Year 3 target):** ~$4.20

## Path to Profitability

```
Month 0 ────── Launch
Month 6 ────── 150+ providers; 5K jobs/month
Month 12 ───── 85K cumulative jobs; first B2B partner live
Month 18 ───── Contribution margin positive per job
Month 22-26 ── UAE operations contribution breakeven
Month 30-34 ── EBITDA breakeven (UAE)
Month 36+ ─── KSA scaling; group profitability path
```

**Key milestones for cost discipline:**
- CAC payback < 3 jobs by Month 12
- Support cost per job < $3 by Month 24
- Provider acquisition cost < $200 by Month 18

## Cost Optimization Levers

| Lever | Impact | Timing |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Reduce CAC via B2B demand | High | Month 6+ |
| Automate verification workflow | Medium | Month 9+ |
| Self-service provider onboarding | Medium | Month 12+ |
| Tiered support (chatbot + human escalation) | Medium | Month 6+ |
| Geographic density before expansion | High | Ongoing |
| Referral over paid acquisition | High | Launch |
| Provider subscription revenue offsets ops | Low–Medium | Month 6+ |

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Launch month cost profile:** Month 1: $320K OpEx ($180K marketing, $70K ops setup, $70K fixed). 3,500 jobs completed. Cost per job: $91—expected pre-scale inefficiency.

**Example 2 — B2B CAC reduction:** Insurer partnership routes 800 jobs/month by Month 10. B2B CAC effectively AED 12 per job vs. AED 95 B2C paid acquisition. Blended CAC drops 22%.

**Example 3 — Verification cost at scale:** Month 1–3: manual verification, $45/provider. Month 9+: batch processing + contractor team, $22/provider. Provider acquisition cost drops from $350 to $210.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Invest heavily in CAC Year 1 | Marketplace density requires demand seeding |
| Field sales for provider acquisition | Digital-only insufficient for tow yard recruitment |
| Fractional CFO Year 1 | Cost control without full-time executive expense |
| Defer KSA office until Month 12 | UAE profitability path first |
| Dispute fund capitalized Day 1 | Trust investment; avoids reactive budgeting |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| CAC exceeds $35/customer | High | B2B channel; referral programs; geo focus |
| OpEx grows faster than revenue | High | Monthly unit economics review; hiring gates |
| Provider incentive costs extend beyond 30 days | Medium | Hard sunset on zero commission |
| Support costs scale linearly | Medium | Self-service; tiered support model |
| KSA expansion doubles OpEx prematurely | High | Milestone-gated expansion budget |

## Assumptions

- Series A raise of $6M–$8M funds 24+ months runway
- Year 1 average 7,000 jobs/month ramping from 3,500 to 12,000
- Payment processing at 2.5% of GMV
- Dubai office costs AED 180K/year co-working scaling to dedicated
- No owned fleet or vehicle capital expenditure

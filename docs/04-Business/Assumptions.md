## Business Examples

**Example 1 — C01 validation:** Pre-launch beta with 500 users: 78% complete first request flow; 62% say they would use over insurer hotline. Assumption trending toward validated; full validation at launch NPS.

**Example 2 — P01 at risk:** Month 4: 25% of providers complain about 18% commission. Field sales interviews reveal 15% is comfort zone. Leadership reviews: maintain 18% with enhanced analytics tier or reduce to 16%? Assumption re-evaluation triggered.

**Example 3 — B01 milestone:** Month −2: Insurer LOI signed with Top-5 UAE carrier. Assumption B01 validated. B04 (live by Month 8) now on critical path.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Document all assumptions explicitly | Series A diligence requirement; enables pivot decisions |
| Tag validation status honestly | "Validated" only with evidence, not hope |
| Failed assumption triggers review, not automatic pivot | Leadership evaluates context |
| Critical assumptions (C01, P01, O01) monitored weekly at launch | Marketplace survival metrics |
| Regulatory assumptions require legal sign-off before launch | Non-negotiable gate |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Core assumptions invalid at launch | Critical | Pre-launch validation sprints; pivot options documented |
| Assumption doc not updated post-launch | Medium | Monthly assumption review in ops meeting |
| Over-optimistic financial assumptions in investor materials | High | Range scenarios; sensitivity analysis |
| Pending assumptions treated as validated internally | High | Strict tagging discipline |
| KSA assumptions applied to UAE planning | Medium | Market-specific assumption sets |

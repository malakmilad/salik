## Partnership Strategy Overview

Salik Assist partnerships serve three strategic goals:

| Goal | Partnership Role |
|------|-----------------|
| **Demand acquisition** | Reduce CAC through embedded distribution |
| **Supply density** | Accelerate verified provider recruitment |
| **Market legitimacy** | Trust transfer via established brands and institutions |

**Partnership philosophy:** Co-create value; avoid exclusivity unless strategically justified; maintain marketplace neutrality.

---

## Demand-Side Partnerships

### Motor Insurers (Priority: Critical)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Value to Salik** | High-volume recurring demand; reduced CAC; trust transfer |
| **Value to partner** | Improved policyholder NPS; SLA reporting; modern dispatch |
| **Model** | B2B2C white-label or co-branded routing |
| **Revenue** | Platform fee + per-dispatch + reduced commission |
| **Target** | 3 signed LOIs Year 1; 1 live by Month 8 |

**Ideal partner profile:** Top 5 UAE motor insurer by comprehensive policy volume; existing roadside benefit in policy; digital transformation mandate.

### Fleet & Rental Operators (Priority: High)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Value to Salik** | Predictable B2B volume; multi-vehicle recurring demand |
| **Value to partner** | Single dispatch partner; downtime reduction; SLA reporting |
| **Model** | Enterprise contract with rate cards |
| **Target** | 2 fleet/rental contracts by Year 2 |

### Car Dealerships & OEMs (Priority: Medium)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Value to Salik** | Post-warranty customer access; new car buyer onboarding |
| **Value to partner** | Customer retention; overflow roadside beyond warranty |
| **Model** | Referral or embedded dispatch in dealer app |
| **Target** | 1 OEM or dealer group pilot by Year 2 |

### Tourism & Hospitality (Priority: Low–Medium)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Value to Salik** | Expat/tourist segment access |
| **Value to partner** | Guest safety service; concierge value-add |
| **Model** | Referral QR codes; hotel concierge integration |
| **Target** | 5 hotel/concierge partners by Year 1 |

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Insurer partnership:** Top-3 UAE insurer embeds Salik Assist in policyholder app. 15,000 roadside events/year routed. Insurer pays AED 10K/month platform fee + AED 10/dispatch. Policyholder NPS on roadside improves from 32 to 58 within 6 months.

**Example 2 — Battery retail chain:** National battery retailer registers 40 mobile technicians as Salik providers. In-store QR codes recruit customers. Retailer sells 25% more batteries from Salik-originated jump-start jobs that escalate to replacement.

**Example 3 — Tow association MOU:** Dubai tow operators association endorses Salik Assist. Hosts recruitment event; 35 providers verified in one month. Association receives AED 50 per verified member as recruitment incentive (within provider referral budget).

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Insurer partnerships highest priority | Volume + trust + CAC reduction |
| Non-exclusive provider partnerships | Supply side adoption |
| No exclusivity with insurers at launch | Avoid demand concentration risk |
| Payment partner: single primary + backup | Reliability for payout SLA |
| Association partnerships over individual mega-providers | Marketplace neutrality |

---

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Insurer partnership delays 12+ months | High | Parallel B2C launch; multiple insurer pipeline |
| Single payment provider dependency | Medium | Backup provider contracted |
| Association endorsement without member conversion | Medium | Direct field sales alongside MOU |
| Partner brand conflict (Salik toll confusion) | Medium | Co-brand guidelines; legal clearance |
| Exclusive demand contract limits marketplace | High | Policy against exclusivity at launch |

## Assumptions

- Payment provider supports 48-hour provider payout
- Tow association willing to endorse non-exclusive platform
- B2B partner sales cycle: 6–9 months average

## About This Repository

Enterprise-level business documentation for **Salik Assist**, a roadside assistance marketplace connecting car owners with verified service providers across the GCC.

**Platform model:** Marketplace only — no owned fleet. Customers receive top-3 provider offers; providers remain independent businesses.

**Service categories:** Car towing, mobile mechanic, mobile electrician, battery replacement, fuel delivery, tire replacement, emergency roadside assistance.

**Documentation scope:**
- **01–10:** Business, market, product strategy, operations, legal summaries, finance, roadmap, appendix
- **11–14:** Execution layer for launch — PRD/BRD, engineering specs, design stubs, launch checklist

## Document Standards

Every document in this repository includes:

- Title, version, status, author, last updated
- Purpose and scope
- Related document cross-references
- Table of contents
- Detailed content with business examples
- Business decisions, risks, assumptions, open questions, next steps

**Review cadence:** Quarterly, or upon material market or regulatory change.

**Change authority:** Product & Strategy Team with functional sign-off (Legal, Finance, Operations as applicable).

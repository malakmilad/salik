## Agreement Overview

The Provider Agreement is executed electronically during [Provider Onboarding](../06-Operations/Provider-Onboarding.md) before activation. Key sections:

| Section | Summary |
|---------|---------|
| Definitions | Provider, job, offer, GMV, commission, service categories |
| Relationship | Independent contractor; not employment |
| Platform access | App, dashboard, matching, payment facilitation |
| Provider duties | Service delivery, licensing, insurance, standards |
| Commercial terms | Commission, subscriptions, incentives |
| Quality & performance | SPS, SLAs, audits |
| Payment | Payout schedule, disputes, chargebacks |
| Term & termination | Duration, suspension, offboarding |
| Legal | Governing law, dispute resolution, indemnification |

**Term:** Rolling 12-month term with auto-renewal unless terminated with 30-day notice.

## Parties & Relationship

### Provider Status

Providers are **independent businesses** — not employees, agents (except limited payment collection), or partners of Salik Assist.

| Element | Provider Control |
|---------|------------------|
| Work schedule | Provider sets availability |
| Pricing | Provider sets rates within platform guardrails |
| Equipment & vehicles | Provider owns/leases |
| Staff | Provider hires, pays, and manages |
| Other platforms | Provider may use competing platforms (non-exclusive default) |
| Service methods | Provider determines how to perform service (within safety standards) |

**Tax & labor:** Provider solely responsible for UAE tax obligations, employee visas, labor law compliance, and business licensing.

## Provider Obligations

### Legal & Licensing
- Maintain valid UAE trade license throughout term
- Maintain valid commercial vehicle insurance (min. AED 1M; AED 2M for Premium Verified)
- Maintain category-appropriate permits (RTA recovery for tow, etc.)
- Maintain valid vehicle registrations for all fleet units in matching pool
- Notify Salik Assist of any license, insurance, or permit change within 48 hours

### Service Delivery
- Respond to offer invitations within 90 seconds (median target)
- Acknowledge accepted jobs within 60 seconds
- Arrive within committed ETA ±15 minutes
- Update status accurately (en route, arrived, in progress, complete)
- Contact customer if ETA changes by >10 minutes
- Perform services competently and safely per [Quality Control](../06-Operations/Quality-Control.md)
- Not discriminate against customers (nationality, gender, location)

### Pricing & Payment
- Set prices within platform guardrails (floor and ceiling by category/zone)
- Obtain in-app customer approval before charges exceeding estimate by >20%
- Accept platform-facilitated payment as primary method
- Not solicit off-platform payment from Salik Assist customers (penalty: suspension)

### Data & Confidentiality
- Use customer data only for job delivery; not retain or market to customers independently
- Not share customer phone number with third parties
- Protect Salik Assist confidential information (matching algorithms, pricing data)

### Brand & Conduct
- Display Salik Assist verification badge accurately
- Not misrepresent affiliation, credentials, or ratings
- Professional conduct toward customers and platform staff

## Commercial Terms

### Commission Structure

| Tier | Commission Rate | Conditions |
|------|-----------------|------------|
| **Standard** | 18% of job value | Default for all verified providers |
| **Preferred** | 17% | SPS ≥75 for 8 consecutive weeks |
| **Elite** | 16% | SPS ≥90 for 12 consecutive weeks |
| **Launch promo** | 0% | First 20 jobs per provider (first 60 days) |

Commission calculated on final job value (including approved surcharges, excluding VAT passed through).

### Provider Subscription (Optional)

| Plan | Monthly Fee (AED) | Benefits |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| **Basic** | 0 | Standard matching |
| **Pro** | 299 | +5% matching boost; weekly analytics |
| **Premium** | 599 | +10% matching boost; priority support; featured badge |

### Surge Pricing
- Platform may apply surge multiplier during high-demand periods (max 2.5×)
- Provider receives 80% of surge premium; platform 20%
- Provider may opt out of surge matching (reduced visibility during surge)

### Cancellation Fee Split
- Provider receives 80% of customer cancellation fees per [Cancellation Policy](../06-Operations/Cancellation-Policy.md)

### B2B Jobs
- Commission on B2B jobs: 12–15% (lower due to volume certainty)
- Rates per B2B master agreement; provider opts into B2B pool

## Liability & Indemnification

### Provider Liability
- Provider solely liable for service delivery, vehicle damage, personal injury, and employee actions
- Provider indemnifies Salik Assist against claims arising from provider service delivery
- Provider maintains adequate insurance and provides proof on request

### Platform Liability
- Salik Assist liable only for platform service failures (payment errors, gross negligence in matching)
- Platform not liable for provider service outcomes (subject to consumer law mediation role)
- Platform liability capped at commission earned on disputed job

### Indemnification
- Provider indemnifies platform against: license violations, insurance lapses, service negligence claims, off-platform payment solicitation, data misuse
- Platform indemnifies provider against: platform payment errors, unauthorized use of provider data

## Termination & Suspension

### Provider-Initiated Termination
- 30-day written notice via provider app
- Complete pending jobs or transfer per ops guidance
- Pending payouts processed per normal schedule
- Salik branding removed within 7 days

### Platform-Initiated Suspension (Temporary)
- Triggers: no-show, safety complaint, license/insurance lapse, SPS <40, fraud investigation
- Duration: 3–30 days depending on severity
- Pending jobs reassigned; provider receives statement of charges

### Platform-Initiated Termination (Permanent)
- Triggers: fraud, document falsification, 3 no-shows in 90 days, criminal conviction, repeated policy violations after suspension
- Pending legitimate payouts processed; fraudulent job payouts forfeited (legal review)
- Provider barred from re-application (permanent for fraud; 12-month cooling for performance)

### Effect of Termination
- Commission on completed jobs through termination date
- Customer data access revoked immediately
- Confidentiality and indemnification survive termination

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Commission on standard job:** Provider completes tow job. Customer charged AED 300 (incl. VAT). Job value for commission: AED 285 (excl. VAT pass-through). Standard commission 18% = AED 51.30. Provider payout: AED 285 − AED 51.30 = AED 233.70 (T+2).

**Example 2 — Suspension for insurance lapse:** Provider insurance expires. Auto-suspension triggered. Provider uploads renewed certificate within 48 hours. Re-verified and reactivated. 2 cancelled offers during suspension; no penalty (system-initiated).

**Example 3 — Off-platform payment violation:** Customer reports provider requested cash payment outside app to "avoid fees." Investigation confirms. First offense: warning + mandatory retraining. Second offense within 90 days: 14-day suspension.

**Example 4 — Elite tier upgrade:** Provider maintains SPS 91 for 12 weeks. Commission drops from 18% to 16%. T+1 payout activated. Monthly earnings increase by ~AED 800 on 40 jobs/month average.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Non-exclusive by default | Provider-friendly; avoids antitrust concerns; reflects market practice |
| 18% standard commission | Market-competitive; supports unit economics |
| Tiered commission for performance | Rewards quality; reduces churn of top providers |
| 0% launch promo (20 jobs) | Supply acquisition; documented as time-limited |
| Provider liable for service outcomes | Marketplace model integrity; insurance requirement |
| 30-day termination notice | Balances provider flexibility with ops stability |
| Off-platform payment prohibition | Protects revenue and dispute evidence |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Provider misclassification as employee | High | Clear agreement terms; provider independence |
| Commission rate rejection by providers | Medium | Launch promo; tiered discounts; demand value proof |
| Indemnification unenforceable | Medium | Legal review; insurance requirements |
| Exclusive agreement demand from top providers | Medium | Non-exclusive default; optional zone exclusivity (TBD) |
| Cross-border provider terms (KSA) | Medium | Localized agreement for expansion |

## Assumptions

- Electronic signature valid for provider agreement in UAE
- 18% commission acceptable to 70%+ of target provider pipeline
- Independent contractor classification defensible under UAE labor law
- Providers maintain required insurance without platform group policy
- Non-exclusive model sufficient for launch supply density

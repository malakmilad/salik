## Agreement Overview

By using Salik Assist, customers agree to terms governing:

- Use of the marketplace platform
- Booking roadside assistance through verified providers
- Payment, cancellation, and refund rules
- Acceptable use and account responsibilities
- Limitation of platform liability

**Effective upon:** First service request submission or account registration, whichever occurs first.

**Governing law:** United Arab Emirates (specific emirate per entity registration — counsel to confirm).

**Language:** Terms available in Arabic and English; English prevails in case of conflict unless UAE consumer law requires otherwise.

---

## Platform Role & Relationship

| Element | Salik Assist Role | What Salik Assist Is NOT |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Marketplace operator | Connects customers with independent providers | Service provider or employer of providers |
| Payment facilitator | Processes payment between customer and provider | Insurer or warranty provider |
| Quality enforcer | Sets standards, verifies providers, handles disputes | Guarantor of service outcome |
| Agent | Limited agent for payment collection on provider behalf | General agent with authority to bind providers beyond platform scope |

**Key legal positioning:** The service contract for roadside assistance is between the **customer and the provider**. Salik Assist facilitates discovery, booking, payment, and dispute resolution under platform terms.

**Provider independence:** Providers set prices within guardrails, own equipment, employ their staff, and maintain their own insurance and licenses.

## Customer Terms Summary

### Eligibility
- Must be 18 years or older (or authorized vehicle operator)
- Must provide accurate location, vehicle, and contact information
- Must have valid payment method (B2C) or authorized partner billing (B2B)

### Customer Obligations
- Provide accurate service request information
- Be present and reachable at service location (or authorize representative)
- Accept or decline price revisions in-app before additional work proceeds
- Pay agreed amount upon service completion
- Treat providers respectfully; report safety concerns immediately
- Not misuse platform (fake requests, fraud, harassment)

### Customer Rights
- Receive up to 3 competitive offers from verified providers
- Track provider en route
- Cancel per [Cancellation Policy](../06-Operations/Cancellation-Policy.md)
- Request refund per [Refund Policy](../06-Operations/Refund-Policy.md)
- Rate and review provider after service
- Contact support 24/7 during active jobs

### Prohibited Customer Conduct
- Submit false or prank requests
- Chargeback abuse or fraudulent refund claims
- Discrimination or harassment of providers
- Circumvent platform to pay provider directly (subject to account suspension)
- Use platform for non-roadside services

## Provider Relationship (Customer Perspective)

Customers understand and agree:

1. **Provider is independent contractor** — not Salik Assist employee
2. **Provider credentials verified** — license, insurance checked at onboarding; customer sees verification badge
3. **Service delivery is provider responsibility** — Salik Assist mediates disputes but does not perform service
4. **Insurance claims** — vehicle damage claims may require provider's commercial insurance; Salik Assist assists with documentation
5. **Direct communication permitted** — customer and provider may communicate for service delivery; platform records in-app messages

## Liability & Disclaimers

### Platform Liability Limits

| Scenario | Platform Responsibility |
|----------|------------------------|
| Provider service quality | Mediate disputes; not liable for provider negligence (subject to consumer law) |
| Provider no-show / SLA breach | Refund/credit per policies; not liable for consequential damages |
| Vehicle damage during service | Assist with provider insurance claim; not direct insurer |
| Personal injury | Report and escalate; provider insurance primary |
| Platform outage | Credit per SLA; not liable for breakdown consequences during outage |
| Third-party content (ratings) | Platform not liable for user-generated content; moderation applied |

### Disclaimer of Warranties
- Platform provided "as is" for marketplace facilitation
- Salik Assist does not warrant uninterrupted availability
- ETA and price estimates are good-faith projections, not guarantees (except where SLA remedies apply)

### Limitation of Damages
- Platform liability capped at fees paid for the specific job in dispute (counsel to confirm enforceability under UAE Consumer Protection Law)
- No liability for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages to extent permitted by law

**Consumer protection note:** UAE Consumer Protection Law may override certain limitation clauses for B2C transactions. Legal counsel must review before publication.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Marketplace liability:** Customer's vehicle scratched during tow. Customer claims against Salik Assist. Terms clarify service contract is with provider. Salik Assist facilitates provider insurance claim, offers dispute mediation, and may issue goodwill credit per policy. Direct liability limited per terms (subject to consumer law).

**Example 2 — Price dispute:** Customer accepts AED 200 estimate. Provider completes service, charges AED 260 without in-app approval. Customer disputes. Refund policy applies: overcharge refunded. Terms supported customer protection without platform admitting service liability.

**Example 3 — Account suspension:** Customer files 3 fraudulent chargebacks in 60 days. Terms permit account suspension. Customer notified; outstanding legitimate job refunds processed separately.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Marketplace intermediary positioning | Avoids transport operator classification; capital-light model |
| Policies incorporated by reference | Operational policies can update without full terms re-sign |
| Arabic + English terms | UAE legal and market requirement |
| 48-hour dispute window in terms | Sets customer expectation; aligns with refund policy |
| Liability cap at job fee | Standard marketplace practice; counsel to validate |
| Prohibit off-platform payment | Protects commission model and dispute evidence trail |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|

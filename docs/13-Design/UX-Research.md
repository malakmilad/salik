## Purpose

Plan lightweight research to de-risk emergency UX before high-fidelity UI.

## Planned Activities

1. **Task analysis** from [Customer Journey](../02-Market-Research/Customer-Journey.md) — stress, one-hand use, poor connectivity
2. **5–8 guerrilla interviews** with Dubai drivers (breakdown recall)
3. **Provider shadowing** — how tow operators take jobs today
4. **Usability tests** on wireframes: request &lt;60s, offer compare, tracking trust

## Research Questions

- What info is mandatory vs deferrable at request time?
- Do users understand top-3 offers vs first-available?
- Guest OTP friction acceptable in panic?
- Arabic copy clarity for fees and ETA?

## Outputs

- Insights memo
- Prioritized UX risks linked to PRD stories
- Input to [Wireframes](./Wireframes.md)

## Revenue Model Overview

Salik Assist generates revenue through five streams:

| Stream | Year 1 Mix | Year 3 Mix | Description |
|--------|--------------|------------|-------------|
| **Commission (take rate)** | 82% | 70% | % of completed job GMV |
| **Provider subscriptions** | 5% | 12% | Pro/Premium tier monthly fees |
| **Surge share** | 4% | 5% | Platform share of surge premium |
| **B2B platform fees** | 7% | 10% | Per-dispatch or monthly fees from insurers/fleets |
| **Promoted listings** | 2% | 3% | Paid provider visibility in competitive zones |

**Primary metric:** Net revenue = GMV × effective take rate + subscription + B2B fees.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Single tow job revenue:** Customer pays AED 280 for tow. Provider commission 18% = AED 50.40 platform revenue. Surge not applicable. Provider payout AED 229.60 (T+2).

**Example 2 — B2B insurance dispatch:** Insurer-routed tow at contracted AED 250. B2B commission 12% = AED 30. Additional B2B platform fee AED 5 per dispatch = AED 35 total platform revenue. Customer zero out-of-pocket.

**Example 3 — Premium provider subscription:** Provider pays AED 599/month for Premium tier. No commission change. If provider completes 60 jobs at AED 200 avg, subscription = 5% of their commission equivalent — worthwhile for matching boost.

**Example 4 — Year 1 Q1 conservative start:** Launch month 1,800 jobs at AED 181 AOV = AED 326K GMV. With 0% promo commission, revenue from subscriptions (20 providers × AED 299 Pro) + 5 B2B pilot jobs = ~AED 26K net revenue.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| 18% standard commission | Balances unit economics with provider acceptance |
| Zero-commission launch promo | Supply acquisition; revenue ramp delayed 2 months |
| B2B at 12% commission | Volume certainty justifies lower rate; anchor partnerships |
| Subscription as Year 1 revenue (small) | Builds habit; meaningful by Year 3 (12% mix) |
| KSA entry Year 3 | UAE proof point before capital-intensive expansion |
| Base case for fundraising | Bear case as sensitivity; bull case as upside narrative |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Slower job volume ramp | High | Provider density targets; marketing spend gates |
| Take rate compression from competition | Medium | Differentiate on quality; B2B lock-in |
| B2B partnerships delayed | Medium | B2C-first revenue; adjust Year 2 forecast |
| Seasonal demand volatility | Medium | Surge pricing; summer marketing push |
| KSA regulatory delay | Medium | Extend UAE-only scenario in bear case |
| Launch promo extends beyond plan | Low | Hard cap: 20 jobs/provider, 60 days |

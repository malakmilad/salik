## Positioning Statement

**For** motorists and fleet operators in the GCC who experience vehicle breakdowns,

**Salik Assist** is the roadside assistance marketplace

**That** delivers verified help with transparent pricing and real choice—in minutes, not hours.

**Unlike** insurer call centers, informal tow networks, or single-service apps,

**Salik Assist** shows you three trusted offers, tracks your rescue live, and guarantees fair resolution—across tow, battery, tire, fuel, and mobile repair.

**Tagline:** *Help on the road, on demand.*

---

## Category Definition

Salik Assist is creating and owning the category:

> **Roadside Assistance Marketplace** — a two-sided platform connecting motorists with multiple verified service providers through competitive offers, real-time dispatch, and guaranteed payment.

We explicitly **do not** position as:

- A tow truck company (we don't own fleets)
- An insurer (we don't underwrite risk)
- A generic "services app" (we are emergency-specialized)
- A ride-hailing extension (breakdowns require different trust and SLA)

**Category education priority:** Many customers don't yet know to search for a "marketplace." Secondary messaging uses familiar analogies: *"Uber for roadside help"* (internal/strategic only—not primary consumer copy due to trademark and nuance).

| Priority | Segment | Core Need | Positioning Angle |
|----------|---------|-----------|-------------------|

| 3 | Independent tow & repair operators | More jobs, guaranteed pay | "Your business. Our customers. Paid in 48 hours." |
| 4 | Motor insurers (B2B2C) | SLA improvement, NPS uplift | "Upgrade your roadside experience without building tech." |
| 5 | Fleet & rental operators | Multi-vehicle dispatch | "One partner. Every emirate. Full reporting." |
| 6 | Women drivers (cross-segment) | Safety + accountability | "See who is coming. Share your trip. Rate every job." |

**Launch focus:** Segments 1, 2, and 3 in Dubai metro. Segment 4 pursued in parallel via partnerships pipeline.

## Messaging Framework

### Primary Message (Consumer)

> When your car stops, don't panic—and don't guess. Salik Assist sends your request to nearby verified pros. Compare three offers, pick your helper, track them live, and pay fairly when the job is done.

### Primary Message (Provider)

> Stop waiting for the phone to ring. Salik Assist brings you qualified jobs in your zone—with upfront customer acceptance, digital payment, and a reputation that grows with every rescue.

### Primary Message (B2B Partner)

> Give your customers the roadside experience they expect in 2026: fast, tracked, and accountable. Salik Assist powers dispatch behind your brand with SLA reporting you can trust.

### Proof Points (All Audiences)

| Proof Point | Evidence |
|-------------|----------|
| Verified providers | License, insurance, background checks before activation |
| Three competitive offers | Unique in UAE roadside market |
| Live tracking | From acceptance to arrival |
| Multi-service | Tow, battery, tire, fuel, mechanic in one app |
| GCC-native | Arabic/English, local payments, corridor knowledge |
| Dispute guarantee | Platform-mediated resolution fund |

## Differentiation Pillars

| Pillar | What It Means | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| **Choice** | Top-3 offers, customer selects | Reduces anxiety; avoids "assigned stranger" fear |
| **Clarity** | Price band before accept | Eliminates #1 complaint in roadside (surprise billing) |
| **Confidence** | Verification badges + ratings | Emergency purchases are trust purchases |
| **Coverage** | Multi-category, one app | Customer doesn't diagnose problem before calling |
| **Speed** | Minutes to offers, not hours on hold | Matches ride/food delivery expectations |
| **Fairness** | Provider independence + guaranteed pay | Sustainable supply side |

## Brand Architecture Considerations

**Current working name:** Salik Assist

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Salik Assist** | Leverages UAE "Salik" familiarity (movement, roads) | Potential confusion with Salik toll operator (unrelated entity) |
| **Independent brand** | Clean trademark; no toll association | Zero awareness; higher CAC |
| **Co-brand with partner** | Instant trust via insurer | Limits consumer brand equity |

**Interim positioning decision:** Proceed as *Salik Assist* with clear sub-line *"Roadside Assistance Marketplace"* until legal/trademark review completes. Avoid implying affiliation with Salik toll systems in customer-facing copy.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Lead with transparency + choice, not price | Race-to-bottom destroys provider supply; trust is defensible |
| Position as marketplace, not tow company | Capital efficiency; legal classification; provider appeal |
| Target urban smartphone users first | Fastest adoption; aligns with digital payment |
| Avoid "cheapest tow" messaging | Attracts wrong customers and providers |
| B2B positioning emphasizes SLA upgrade | Insurers don't need another vendor—they need better NPS |
| Arabic-first messaging in UAE launch | Local relevance; expat segment still served in English |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| "Salik" toll brand confusion | Medium | Legal review; clarifying tagline; distinct visual identity |
| "Uber for roadside" oversimplifies | Low | Internal analogy only; emphasize verification differences |
| Positioning promises exceed launch coverage | High | Geo-gated marketing; honest availability messaging |
| Competitor copies top-3 offer messaging | Medium | First-mover brand building; provider exclusivity in zones |
| B2B and B2C messaging conflict | Medium | Separate playbooks; unified proof points |

## Assumptions

- "Transparency" and "choice" resonate more than "speed" alone in customer research
- Providers respond to "guaranteed payment" more than "grow your business" abstractions
- Insurers will co-brand before Salik consumer brand is widely known
- Category term "roadside assistance marketplace" requires education over 12–18 months
- GCC motorists familiar with ride-hailing will map expectations to Salik Assist quickly

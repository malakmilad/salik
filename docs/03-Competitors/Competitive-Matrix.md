## Scoring Methodology

Each competitor is scored **1–5** per dimension:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Absent or very weak |
| 2 | Basic / inconsistent |
| 3 | Adequate / industry standard |
| 4 | Strong / differentiated |
| 5 | Best-in-class / market leading |

Scores reflect **UAE market as of July 2026** based on desk research, customer interviews, and provider feedback. Weighted total uses marketplace-relevant weights.

**Dimension weights:**

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Service breadth | 20% |
| Geographic coverage (UAE) | 15% |
| Pricing transparency | 20% |
| Provider verification & trust | 15% |
| Customer choice / competitive offers | 15% |
| B2B / insurer channel | 10% |
| Provider economics & payment speed | 5% |

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Matrix-driven messaging:** Marketing uses the pricing transparency row (Salik 5 vs. insurer 1) as core ad copy: *"See three prices before you choose—not a surprise bill after a 40-minute wait."*

**Example 2 — Partnership pitch:** Partnerships team presents B2B matrix to an insurer showing SLA reporting at 5 vs. their internal 3, proposing Salik as upgrade layer without replacing brand-facing hotline.

**Example 3 — Provider recruitment:** Field sales shows providers the "Provider economics" column—commission burden of 3 offset by payment guarantee of 5 and lead volume of 4 vs. waiting for WhatsApp referrals at 2.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Publish matrix internally, not externally | Avoid provoking incumbent price or partnership retaliation pre-launch |
| Re-score quarterly | Marketplace dynamics shift quickly with funding events |
| Weight transparency and choice highest | Aligns with customer pain points validated in research |
| Accept B2B score of 4 until first insurer LOI signed | Honest investor narrative; milestone to improve |
| Do not chase informal network geographic score | Ubiquity without trust is not our target segment |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Scores perceived as biased by investors | Medium | Document methodology; third-party validation interviews |
| Competitor improves transparency before launch | Medium | Speed to market; patent/trademark brand narrative |
| Over-indexing on matrix vs. execution | Medium | Matrix informs GTM; operations delivers scores |
| Insurer scores improve via insurtech acquisition | High | Accelerate partnership pipeline |

## Assumptions

- Scoring reflects pre-launch Salik Assist design intent, not live performance
- Competitor capabilities are publicly observable or interview-validated
- Weights align with customer stated priorities (transparency > breadth > price)
- Category apps remain single-city through Year 1
- Insurer B2B score of 5 holds for existing relationships; new digital entrants score lower

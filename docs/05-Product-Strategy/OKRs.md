## OKR Framework

| Element | Salik Assist Convention |
|---------|-------------------------|
| **Cadence** | Quarterly objectives; monthly check-ins |
| **Scoring** | 0.0–1.0 per Key Result; 0.7 = strong |
| **North Star alignment** | Every objective must trace to SCAJ or its drivers |
| **Count** | 3–5 objectives per quarter; 2–4 KRs each |
| **Ownership** | Single DRI per Key Result |
| **Transparency** | Published internally; board summary monthly |

## OKR Scoring and Review

### Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0.0–0.3 | Failed; significant miss |
| 0.4–0.6 | Partial; needs intervention |
| 0.7–0.9 | Strong; on track |
| 1.0 | Fully achieved (rare; may indicate sandbagging) |

### Review Calendar

| Meeting | Frequency | Participants |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| KR check-in | Monthly | DRI + functional lead |
| OKR scoring | End of quarter | Exec team |
| Board OKR summary | Monthly | CEO + board |
| All-hands OKR readout | Quarterly | Company |

### OKR Changes Mid-Quarter

Allowed only for:
- Regulatory blockers
- Force majeure (e.g., city-wide restrictions)
- Board-directed pivot

Changes require CEO approval and documented rationale.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Liquidity KR failure response:** Q4 KR1.3 (Liquidity Index ≥55%) scores 0.5 at mid-quarter. Exec redirects field sales from Abu Dhabi scouting to Marina zone; pauses tire category beta. Q4 ends at 0.72.

**Example 2 — B2B KR acceleration:** Partnerships misses Q3 LOI target. Q4 adds interim KR: "3 insurer workshops completed" to unblock Q1 pilot. LOI signed Week 2 Q4.

**Example 3 — Sandbagging detection:** Operations hits 1.0 on provider sign-ups (150) but Liquidity Index 0.4—reveals providers signed but not active. Q1 adds KR: "Active providers completing ≥1 job/week: 80."

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| SCAJ-linked OKRs at company level | Reinforces North Star culture |
| Pre-launch Q3 has zero marketing spend OKR | Supply and product validation first |
| Liquidity OKRs owned by Operations | Supply density is primary liquidity lever |
| 0.7 = success threshold | Prevents sandbagging and burnout from 1.0-only culture |
| B2B OKRs from Q4 soft launch | Partnerships need live jobs to prove value |
| Category expansion OKRs gated on liquidity | Principle 8: Liquidity Over Novelty |

## Risks

| Risk | OKR Impact |
|------|------------|
| Premature public launch pressure | Q1 SCAJ missed; trust damaged |
| OKR overload (too many KRs) | Diluted focus; score everything 0.5 |
| Siloed OKRs | Growth drives requests without ops liquidity |
| Gaming provider sign-ups | KR1.1 green, liquidity red |
| B2B partner delays | Partnership OKRs slip 1–2 quarters |

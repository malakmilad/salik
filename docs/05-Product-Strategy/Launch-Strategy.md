## Launch Vision

**Launch Salik Assist in Dubai as the region's first transparent top-3 roadside marketplace—proving 3,500+ Successfully Completed Assisted Jobs in beta, then scaling to public launch with ≥65% Liquidity Index in core zones and customer trust scores that support national expansion.**

Launch is not a single date—it is a **gated sequence** protecting brand trust in emergency contexts.

---

## Launch Principles

1. **Supply before demand** — Never market to customers where providers cannot respond
2. **Gates over calendars** — Public launch date moves if readiness gates fail
3. **Zone depth over city breadth** — Win Marina before Mirdif
4. **Honest liquidity** — Disclose when fewer than three providers are available
5. **Human backup** — Support and ops staffed for launch surge, not ticket-only
6. **Measure SCAJ from day one** — Beta jobs count toward learning, not vanity

## Phase 0: Pre-Launch Readiness

### Objectives

- 150 verified providers onboarded
- Launch zone map finalized with coverage analysis
- Mystery shopper program complete (50 jobs)
- Legal, compliance, and insurance frameworks signed off
- B2B LOI or pilot agreement with anchor partner
- App store submissions in review (staging → production)

### Key Activities

| Workstream | Activities | Owner |
|------------|------------|-------|
| **Supply** | Field sales, verification, provider app training | Operations |
| **Product** | MVP feature complete, bilingual QA, beta build | Product |
| **Partnerships** | Insurer/fleet LOI negotiations | Partnerships |
| **Marketing** | Creative production, press kit, referral mechanics | Marketing |
| **Support** | Scripts, dispute SOP, 24/7 launch roster | Support |
| **Legal** | Marketplace terms, provider agreements, privacy | Legal |

### Phase 0 Exit Criteria

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Verified providers | ≥150 |
| Zone coverage (3+ providers / 15 km) | ≥90% of launch map |
| Mystery shopper jobs | ≥50 completed |
| TT3O (median, test jobs) | ≤6 minutes |
| Provider offer response rate (drills) | ≥55% |
| Anchor B2B LOI | ≥1 signed |
| Launch readiness review | Exec sign-off |

## Phase 1: Closed Beta (Soft Launch)

### Objectives

- Prove end-to-end marketplace with real customers and providers
- Achieve 3,500 cumulative SCAJ
- Validate top-3 model under moderate load
- Iterate on support, disputes, and provider payouts
- Prepare app store and marketing for public launch

### Beta Cohort Design

| Cohort | Size | Access Method |
|--------|------|---------------|
| **Internal + friends** | 200 | Invite codes |
| **Provider referrals** | 500 | Provider-shared codes |
| **Waitlist (geo-verified Dubai)** | 2,000 | Sequential invite by zone |
| **B2B pilot** | Partner policyholders | Partner app integration |
| **Mystery shoppers** | Ongoing | Ops-contracted |

**Beta cap:** 5,000 registered users maximum until public launch gate met.

### Beta Operating Rules

- Service limited to tow + battery in defined zones
- Support SLA: 5-minute first response
- Disputes escalated to ops lead within 2 hours
- Weekly beta retrospective with product, ops, support
- NPS survey every 100 SCAJ

### Phase 1 Exit Criteria (Public Launch Gate)

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Cumulative SCAJ | ≥3,500 |
| Fill rate | ≥88% |
| Liquidity Index (beta zones) | ≥60% (stretch 65%) |
| CSAT | ≥4.2 |
| Dispute rate | ≤6% |
| Provider 30-day retention | ≥65% |
| Critical launch blockers | Zero open P0 issues |
| App store approval | iOS + Android live |

## Phase 2: Public Launch (Dubai Core)

### Launch Geography (Day 1)

Public launch activates **only** zones meeting beta liquidity thresholds:

- Sheikh Zayed Road (Dubai)
- Dubai Marina / JBR / JLT
- Downtown / Business Bay
- Dubai Mall corridor
- Selected E611 / E44 exits

**Deferred at launch:** Mirdif, DSO, Jebel Ali (unless gate met pre-launch)

### Launch Week Objectives

| Metric | Launch Week Target |
|--------|-------------------|
| SCAJ | 1,200+ |
| Liquidity Index | ≥65% |
| App installs | 8,000+ |
| Press coverage | 5+ tier-1 mentions |
| Support SLA | 95% within 5 min |

### Launch Week Activity Calendar

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| **D-7** | Press embargo briefings; influencer briefings |
| **D-3** | Provider launch rally (Al Quoz); final ops staffing |
| **D-1** | War room setup; executive on-call roster |
| **D0** | PR wire; app store featuring push; paid ads live (geo-fenced) |
| **D+1** | Monitor Liquidity Index hourly; adjust ad spend by zone |
| **D+3** | First customer story publish; referral push |
| **D+7** | Launch retrospective; week-1 board summary |

### Launch Day War Room

**Participants:** GM, Product, Operations, Support, Marketing, Partnerships  
**Cadence:** Hourly standup 6 AM–10 PM launch day; 4-hour cadence Days 2–7  
**Monitors:** SCAJ real-time, Liquidity Index by zone, support queue, app store reviews, provider response rate

## Phase 3: Post-Launch Stabilization

### Objectives (Weeks 2–20 post-launch)

- Scale to 7,500 monthly SCAJ by M6 post-launch
- Expand to secondary Dubai zones
- Add tire + mobile mechanic categories
- Achieve contribution margin positive trajectory
- Sign second B2B contract

### Stabilization Priorities

| Priority | Success Metric |
|----------|----------------|
| Fix liquidity deserts | No zone below 55% Liquidity Index for 2+ weeks |
| Reduce disputes | ≤4% dispute rate by M3 post-launch |
| Accelerate payouts | ≤48-hour provider payout median |
| Improve repeat rate | ≥10% 90-day repeat by M6 |
| Scale efficient channels | Referral ≥15% of new requests |

## Launch Readiness Gates

### Gate Summary

```
Phase 0 ──[150 providers, 50 mystery jobs]──► Phase 1 Beta
Phase 1 ──[3,500 SCAJ, 88% fill, 60% liquidity]──► Phase 2 Public
Phase 2 ──[4 weeks stable ops]──► Phase 3 Scale
```

### Red/Yellow/Green Launch Decision

| Status | Condition | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Green** | All exit criteria met | Proceed to next phase |
| **Yellow** | 1–2 criteria missed by <10% | 2-week delay; remediate |
| **Red** | Critical criteria missed | Halt; exec review; no public marketing |

**Critical (Red) criteria:** Fill rate <80%; dispute rate >10%; provider response <45%; safety incident unresolved.

## Launch Day Playbook

### Customer Journey (Launch Day)

1. Customer sees ad / PR / referral → downloads app
2. Onboarding ≤60 seconds → requests help
3. Receives up to 3 offers within 5 minutes
4. Accepts → tracks provider → completes → rates
5. Referral prompt (post-rating, not pre-rescue)

### Support Playbook Highlights

| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| No offers in 10 min | Ops manual provider ping; customer credit if unresolved |
| Provider no-show | Auto-refund + re-dispatch priority |
| Price dispute | Support reviews estimate vs. final; partial credit if warranted |
| Safety concern | Immediate escalation; provider suspend pending review |
| App crash during request | Support callback within 3 min; manual dispatch backup |

### Provider Playbook Highlights

- Launch day bonus: AED 50 per completed job (first 48 hours, cap 5/provider)
- Dedicated WhatsApp ops line for provider issues
- Real-time zone demand heatmap shared hourly

## Contingency and Rollback

| Scenario | Trigger | Response |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Liquidity collapse** | Index <50% for 24h | Pause paid ads; surge provider bonuses |
| **Safety incident** | Verified serious complaint | Suspend provider; PR statement; exec review |
| **Overwhelming demand** | Queue >15 min TTFO | Waitlist message; expand provider pings |
| **App store removal** | Policy violation | Web fallback request flow; expedited fix |
| **Provider strike threat** | Mass non-response | Commission review; emergency ops negotiation |

**Rollback scope:** Geographic pause (zone-level), not full product shutdown unless safety/legal mandate.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Gate delay:** Phase 1 ends with 3,200 SCAJ and Liquidity Index 58%. Public launch delayed 3 weeks. Field sales recruits 20 Marina battery providers. Re-hit gate at 3,600 SCAJ and 62% liquidity; public launch approved with Marina-only ad spend initially.

**Example 2 — Launch day surge:** D0 SZR request volume 3× forecast. TTFO rises to 6 min. War room activates surge provider bonus. TTFO returns to 3.5 min by D+2. SCAJ 1,450 for launch week.

**Example 3 — Beta dispute learning:** Beta dispute rate 7.2% driven by price variance on tow distance. Product adds distance surcharge calculator to offer cards. Public launch dispute rate 4.8% in week 1.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Gated launch vs. big-bang UAE | Protect trust; prove liquidity |
| 5,000 user beta cap | Controlled load for iteration |
| Tow + battery only at launch | Focus supply; highest frequency |
| Launch week provider bonus | Ensure response rate surge |
| Zone-level rollback capability | Surgical response vs. national pause |
| Mystery shoppers before beta | Baseline TTFO/TTA before real customers |
| Public launch requires app store live | Emergency category needs trusted distribution |

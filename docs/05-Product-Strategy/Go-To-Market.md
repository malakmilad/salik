## GTM Overview

Salik Assist enters the UAE roadside assistance market as a **two-sided marketplace** with a differentiated **top-3 provider offer** model. GTM strategy follows a **supply-first, liquidity-gated demand** sequence:

1. **Recruit and verify providers** in Dubai launch zones
2. **Beta with controlled demand** (invite-only, mystery shoppers, partner pilots)
3. **Public consumer launch** in zones with proven liquidity
4. **Scale channels** only where Liquidity Index ≥65%
5. **Expand geography** per [Expansion Strategy](./Expansion-Strategy.md)

**GTM North Star alignment:** All channels optimize for **Successfully Completed Assisted Jobs**, not vanity acquisition.

## Market Entry Thesis

| Belief | GTM Implication |
|--------|-----------------|
| Motorists distrust unknown tow numbers | Lead with verification and choice messaging |
| Insurer hotlines are slow but trusted | Partner with insurers; compete on speed + transparency |
| Providers want demand, not employment | Zero-commission launch period; earnings case studies |
| Dubai density enables liquidity | Hyper-local launch, not UAE-wide day one |
| Arabic + English equally required | Bilingual campaigns from first touchpoint |
| Emergency intent is high-stress | Simple CTA: "Three offers in minutes" |

## Value Proposition and Messaging

### Customer Value Proposition

**Headline:** *Three trusted rescue options in minutes.*

**Supporting pillars:**
- **Choice:** Compare ETA, price, and ratings—not one unknown truck
- **Trust:** Verified licenses, insurance, and background checks
- **Visibility:** Track your provider live on the map
- **Fair price:** Know the estimate before you accept
- **Pay securely:** Card, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay—no cash haggling

### Provider Value Proposition

**Headline:** *More jobs. Guaranteed payment. Your business.*

**Supporting pillars:**
- Qualified demand delivered to your phone
- No payment chase—platform settles
- Build reputation that brings repeat customers
- Keep your independence—no fleet employment

### Messaging Guardrails

- Never promise "fastest in UAE" without zone-specific data
- Always disclose when fewer than three providers are available
- Avoid fear-mongering; emphasize empowerment and clarity
- Distinguish Salik Assist from Salik toll where brand confusion exists

### Channel Sequencing

| Phase | Active Channels |
|-------|-----------------|
| Pre-launch | Provider field sales only; no consumer spend |
| Soft launch | Referral, invite codes, mystery shoppers, partner pilot |
| Public launch | ASO, performance, PR, offline QR in launch zones |
| Scale | Full mix; reallocate from high-CAC to referral/B2B monthly |

### Referral Program (Launch Design)

- **Customer:** AED 25 credit after referee's first completed job
- **Referee:** AED 25 off first job
- **Provider referral:** AED 200 bonus after referred provider completes 10 jobs
- Cap: 10 referrals/month/customer to limit fraud

## Provider Acquisition Strategy

Supply GTM precedes demand GTM.

### Provider Target Profile

- Licensed tow operators, mobile mechanics, battery specialists
- 1–20 vehicle fleets (SME focus at launch)
- Operating within 20 km of Dubai launch zones
- Willing to accept digital dispatch and card settlement

### Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Tactic |
|---------|--------|
| **Field sales** | Tow yards in Al Quoz, Jebel Ali, Deira industrial |
| **Battery retailers** | Partnership for technician network onboarding |
| **Trade associations** | RTA-licensed operator outreach |
| **Provider referrals** | Bonus for successful provider introductions |
| **Insurer introductions** | Approved vendor lists from B2B partners |

### Provider Launch Incentives

| Incentive | Duration | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| 0% commission | First 60 days / first 20 jobs | Reduce adoption friction |
| Guaranteed minimum earnings | First 30 days (qualified providers) | De-risk trial |
| Free verification onboarding | Launch period | Accelerate supply |
| Priority zone placement | Early joiners in under-covered zones | Geographic balance |

**Target:** 150 verified providers pre-launch; 120 active (≥1 SCAJ/30 days) by M3 public launch.

## B2B Go-To-Market

### Ideal First Partner Profile

- Regional motor insurer or mid-size fleet operator
- Pain: high roadside dispatch cost, poor visibility, customer complaints
- Willingness to pilot 200–500 jobs before enterprise contract

### B2B Sales Motion

1. **Identify** — Partnerships team targets 15 qualified accounts
2. **Discovery** — Audit current roadside cost, SLA, and customer complaints
3. **Pilot proposal** — 90-day pilot with defined zones (Dubai), categories (tow + battery)
4. **Prove** — SCAJ, SLA adherence, cost per dispatch vs. baseline
5. **Contract** — Annual agreement with volume tiers and platform fees

### B2B Value Proposition

| Partner Benefit | Proof Point |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Cost predictability | Pre-negotiated rate caps |
| Audit trail | Job-level reporting |
| Customer satisfaction | CSAT and NPS per partner |
| Faster dispatch | Median TTFO ≤3 min in coverage zones |
| Top-3 choice | Reduced complaint rate vs. auto-assign |

**GTM constraint:** B2B jobs must preserve customer choice among in-network providers where contract permits.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Zone-gated performance marketing:** Growth team proposes UAE-wide Facebook campaign. GTM lead blocks—Sharjah not in launch geography and Liquidity Index N/A. Campaign runs geo-fenced to Marina + SZR only. CAC $19, Liquidity Index holds at 67%.

**Example 2 — Provider field sales:** Field rep signs Al Quoz tow company (8 trucks). Onboards 8 driver profiles. Zone Coverage Score for adjacent highway exit rises from 2 to 5 providers within 15 km. TT3O improves 1.2 minutes.

**Example 3 — Insurer B2B pilot:** Regional insurer embeds "Roadside Help" in policy app. 200 pilot jobs over 90 days. Cost per dispatch 12% below call-center baseline. Customer CSAT 4.5. Contract signed for Year 2 volume tier.

**Example 4 — Highway QR:** QR sticker on SZR rest area signage: "Stranded? Get 3 offers in minutes." Scans convert at 8% to app install; 35% of installs request within 30 days (high intent). Offline CAC $14.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Supply-first GTM | Liquidity required for top-3 promise |
| Dubai zone gating for paid demand | Prevents hollow national brand |
| Zero-commission provider launch period | Supply adoption critical path |
| Referral as primary organic channel | Emergencies are word-of-mouth moments |
| B2B pilot before enterprise scale | Prove SLA and cost before multi-year contract |
| Bilingual GTM from day one | UAE market requirement |
| Pay-per-use vs. subscription at launch | Lower adoption barrier for first job |

## Risks

| Risk | GTM Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Provider recruitment shortfall | Cannot launch publicly | Extend pre-launch; narrow zones |

## Mission Statement

**We connect motorists in distress with trusted roadside professionals—fast, fairly, and transparently—while helping independent providers build sustainable businesses.**

---

## Extended Mission

Salik Assist exists because a stranded driver should never have to guess who to call, whether they'll arrive, or what they'll pay. We build the marketplace layer that turns chaos into choice: verified help, visible ETAs, secure payments, and accountability on both sides.

We serve:

- **Motorists** who need immediate, reliable assistance
- **Independent providers** who deserve digital demand and fair compensation
- **Partners** (insurers, fleets, rentals) who need dependable dispatch at scale

We succeed when every completed job makes the next breakdown less frightening—for customers and for the professionals who show up.

## Core Values

| Value | Meaning | Behavioral Standard |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **Safety First** | Human safety outweighs speed and revenue | Never match unverified providers; escalate unsafe situations immediately |
| **Radical Transparency** | Price, ETA, and credentials visible before commitment | No hidden fees; dispute processes documented publicly |
| **Provider Respect** | Providers are partners, not commodities | Fair commission; timely payout; appeal paths for ratings |
| **Reliability** | Promises kept under stress | SLAs monitored; failures trigger remediation, not excuses |
| **Regional Integrity** | Built for GCC realities | Bilingual support; local payment; cultural sensitivity |
| **Accountability** | Own outcomes end-to-end | Platform facilitates resolution even when provider is at fault |

## Stakeholder Promises

### To Customers

- Verified providers only on the platform
- Multiple offers within minutes for standard requests
- Real-time tracking from acceptance to arrival
- Secure payment only after service delivery (except pre-auth holds)
- 24/7 support access for active jobs

### To Providers

- Qualified leads in your service area
- Clear fee structure and payout schedule
- Dispute process with evidence standards
- Tools to manage availability and service categories
- No exclusivity requirement at launch (subject to B2B contract exceptions)

### To Partners

- Audit-ready job records and SLA reporting
- Pre-negotiated rate cards where contracted
- Dedicated account management at scale
- Co-branded customer experience options

### To Employees

- Mission-driven work with measurable impact on road safety
- Data-informed decisions; no "move fast and break people" culture in emergency contexts

## Mission in Practice

Every business decision should pass the **Stranded Driver Test**:

> *If I were alone on a highway at midnight with my family in the car, would this decision make me feel safer, clearer, and more in control?*

If no, reconsider—even if it improves short-term margin.

---

## Business Examples

**Mission-aligned:** A customer reports an provider arriving in an unmarked vehicle. Operations suspends the provider pending document re-verification—even though demand is high that evening.

**Mission misalignment (avoided):** Listing unverified providers to inflate supply numbers for an investor demo. Rejected: violates Safety First and destroys trust.

**Provider respect:** Introducing a 48-hour payout cycle with instant payout option at small fee—balancing provider cash flow needs with platform fraud controls.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Mission Link |
|----------|--------------|
| Mandatory provider verification | Safety First; customer promise |
| Top-3 offers instead of blind dispatch | Radical Transparency |
| 24/7 human escalation path at launch | Reliability in emergencies |
| Arabic customer support from day one | Regional Integrity |
| Public cancellation and refund policies | Accountability |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact on Mission |
|------|-------------------|
| Growth pressure compromising verification | Erodes core trust promise |
| Provider exploitation perception | Damages supply-side partnership |
| Support understaffing during spikes | Fails Reliability under peak stress |
| Mission drift into general logistics | Confuses brand and dilutes roadside expertise |

---

## Assumptions

- Team members can be recruited who prioritize safety over vanity metrics
- Mission alignment can be maintained through governance as investor pressure increases
- Values are enforceable through policies, not merely poster statements

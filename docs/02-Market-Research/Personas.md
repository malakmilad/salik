## Demand Persona: Sarah — The Daily Commuter

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 32 |
| Location | Dubai Marina; commutes to DIFC daily |
| Nationality | Lebanese expat |
| Vehicle | 2021 Toyota RAV4 |
| Income | AED 18,000/month |
| Insurance | Comprehensive with roadside add-on |
| Tech comfort | High — uses Careem, Talabat, Apple Pay daily |

### Goals

- Get to work and back without disruption
- Resolve breakdowns quickly without missing meetings
- Know the price before committing to a service
- Feel safe waiting for help, especially evenings

### Frustrations

- Called insurer hotline once; waited 35 minutes on hold
- Tow truck arrived without clear pricing; paid AED 400 (felt overcharged)
- Could not track provider location; stood in parking lot unsure if help was coming
- Husband recommended a garage contact but number was unreachable

### Behaviors

- Searches Google "tow truck near me" in emergencies
- Trusts apps with ratings and upfront pricing
- Will pay AED 20–30 more for 10 minutes faster ETA
- Shares positive experiences on Instagram Stories

### Salik Assist Value

Top-3 offers with ETA and price in under 2 minutes. Live tracking. Pay by card. Rate provider. Insurance integration for covered events.

### Quote

> "I don't have time to negotiate on the side of the road. Show me three options, I pick one, done."

## Demand Persona: Raj — The Expat Without a Network

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 28 |
| Location | JLT, Dubai |
| Nationality | Indian expat |
| Vehicle | 2018 Nissan Sunny |
| Income | AED 9,500/month |
| Insurance | Third-party (no roadside coverage) |
| Tech comfort | High — price comparison shopper |

### Goals

- Find affordable, honest roadside help without "local connections"
- Avoid being overcharged as a foreigner
- Fix car and return to work same day
- Build a go-to solution for future emergencies

### Frustrations

- Tow truck driver quoted AED 500; colleague said it should be AED 200
- No receipts; cash-only payment
- Language barrier with some providers
- WhatsApp group recommendations are unreliable

### Behaviors

- Compares 3+ options before any purchase over AED 100
- Reads app reviews carefully (4.5+ minimum)
- Uses price comparison for insurance, flights, electronics
- Refers friends to money-saving services

### Salik Assist Value

Transparent competitive offers eliminate haggling. Verified providers with ratings. Digital receipt. English and Hindi support. Lowest-price option visible in top-3.

### Quote

> "I just want the same price a local would pay. Don't make me guess."

## Demand Persona: Fatima — The Highway Traveler

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 41 |
| Location | Abu Dhabi; drives to Dubai weekly |
| Nationality | Emirati |
| Vehicle | 2023 Lexus LX |
| Income | AED 45,000/month |
| Insurance | Premium comprehensive |
| Tech comfort | Medium — uses smartphone but prefers simplicity |

### Goals

- Get off the highway safely as fast as possible
- Ensure luxury vehicle is handled properly (flatbed)
- Notify family of her location and status
- Minimize time stranded in heat

### Frustrations

- Blowout on E11 near Exit 45; felt unsafe standing outside vehicle
- Insurer dispatch took 50+ minutes to assign provider
- Previous tow used wheel-lift on AWD vehicle (caused damage anxiety)

- Calls husband or brother first in emergencies
- Prefers Arabic interface option
- Will not haggle; wants fastest verified help

### Salik Assist Value

Highway-priority matching. Verified flatbed providers for luxury/AWD. Live location sharing with family. Arabic UI. Fastest ETA highlighted in offers.

### Quote

> "On the highway, I don't care about saving AED 50. I care about getting safe help in 15 minutes."

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 45 |
| Location | Dubai Investment Park |
| Role | Operations Manager, 120-vehicle delivery fleet |
| Company | Regional e-commerce logistics |
| Tech comfort | Medium — uses fleet management dashboard |

### Goals

- Minimize vehicle downtime (AED 500+/hour cost)
- Centralized dispatch for all drivers
- Monthly reporting on breakdown frequency and costs
- Predictable SLA with service partners

Fleet dashboard. Multi-vehicle dispatch. SLA reporting. Consolidated billing. Driver app with one-tap request. B2B pricing.

### Quote

> "I need my drivers to tap one button—not call me, not call random tow trucks."

## Demand Persona: Noura — The Insurance Product Manager

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 36 |
| Location | Dubai (DIFC insurer HQ) |
| Role | Head of Motor Claims & Assistance |
| Company | Mid-tier UAE insurer (300K policies) |
| Tech comfort | High — evaluates InsurTech partnerships |

### Goals

- Reduce cost per roadside event by 15–20%
- Improve policyholder NPS on assistance experience
- Reduce fraud in towing claims
- Modernize assistance offering for competitive differentiation

### Frustrations

- Legacy assistance contract with 45-min average dispatch time
- Policyholders complain about lack of transparency
- Per-event costs rising 8% annually
- Digital-native competitors offering better assistance UX

### Behaviors

- Issues RFP for assistance partners every 2–3 years
- Measures dispatch time, CSAT, cost per event, complaint rate
- Prefers partnership over build (no internal dev budget)
- Requires regulatory compliance documentation

### Salik Assist Value

White-label or API-integrated dispatch. Verified provider network. Real-time tracking for policyholders. Per-dispatch pricing below legacy costs. Analytics dashboard.

### Quote

> "Our policyholders compare us to Careem. Our roadside experience can't feel like it's from 2010."

## Supply Persona: Ahmed — The Tow Operator

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 38 |
| Location | Al Quoz, Dubai |
| Business | 4-truck tow operation (owner-operator) |
| Revenue | AED 35,000–50,000/month |
| Fleet utilization | ~55% |
| Tech comfort | Low-medium — uses WhatsApp and Google Maps |

### Goals

- Fill idle truck hours with paid jobs
- Get paid reliably and quickly (cash flow is tight)
- Build reputation to win insurer contracts
- Avoid non-paying customers and price disputes

### Frustrations

- Sits idle 3–4 hours/day waiting for phone calls
- Customers cancel after he's en route
- Insurance companies pay after 60–90 days
- Informal operators undercut his prices by 30%

### Behaviors

- Relies on garage referrals and roadside visibility
- Accepts cash and bank transfer
- Resists apps that charge >20% commission
- Will trial new platform if first 10 jobs pay on time

### Salik Assist Value

Qualified job alerts on phone. Guaranteed payment within 48 hours. Customer can't cancel after acceptance. Rating system attracts premium jobs. 15% commission at launch (promotional).

### Quote

> "Give me real jobs, pay me on time, and I'll give you 15%. But don't waste my time with fake requests."

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Persona intersection:** Fatima (D3) experiences a highway blowout. Salik Assist serves her urgency need (fastest ETA) and Sarah's (D1) need (price transparency) through the same top-3 interface—Fatima selects fastest; Sarah would select best value.

**Example 2 — B2B2C persona chain:** Noura (D6) signs insurer partnership. Raj (D2), the insurer's policyholder, taps "Roadside Help" in insurer app. Ahmed (S1) receives and fulfills the job. Three personas, one transaction.

**Example 3 — Supply persona onboarding:** Ahmed (S1) skeptical of 18% commission. Salik Assist offers 0% for first 30 days. Ahmed completes 22 jobs in month one; average revenue per job AED 280; net lift of AED 1,400 vs. idle time. Converts to paid tier.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Persona Driver |
|----------|----------------|

| Highway corridor priority | Fatima — safety-critical context |
| Arabic + English UI at launch | Fatima, Ahmed |
| 0% commission launch period for providers | Ahmed — trial conversion |
| Insurer partnership before rental B2B | Noura before James — volume and trust |
| Verification badge prominent in provider profiles | Fatima, Raj — trust in emergencies |

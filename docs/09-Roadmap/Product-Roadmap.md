## Roadmap Vision

Salik Assist evolves through four phases from Dubai MVP to GCC regional marketplace leader:

```
MVP → Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3
Prove   Expand    Deepen    Scale
```

| Phase | Duration | Core Question Answered |
|-------|----------|------------------------|
| **MVP** | Months 1–3 | Does the marketplace work? |
| **Phase 1** | Months 4–18 | Can we expand categories, cities, and B2B? |
| **Phase 2** | Months 19–30 | Can we dominate UAE and build retention? |
| **Phase 3** | Months 31–48 | Can we win KSA and become regional? |

## Roadmap Dependencies & Gates

| Gate | Criteria | Unlocks |
|------|----------|---------|
| **MVP → Phase 1** | 3K jobs/mo; 85% completion; 60% provider retention | Category + geo expansion |
| **Phase 1 → Phase 2** | 18K jobs/mo; AED 750K rev/mo; 2K B2B jobs/mo | Subscription; all emirates |
| **Phase 2 → Phase 3** | 30K UAE jobs/mo; cash flow positive; KSA entity ready | KSA launch |
| **Riyadh → Jeddah** | 5K Riyadh jobs/mo; 200+ Riyadh providers | Jeddah launch |
| **KSA → Secondary GCC** | 15K KSA jobs/mo; dedicated country manager | Bahrain/Qatar |

**Kill criteria:** If MVP gate fails (Day 90), pause Phase 1; extend MVP optimization 60 days before reassessing.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Roadmap gate decision (M3):** Day 90 review shows 3,400 jobs/month, 87% completion, 68% provider retention. All gates passed. Phase 1 approved. Tire category launch scheduled M5. Abu Dhabi field sales start M7.

**Example 2 — Subscription inflection (M24):** 45,000 subscribers at AED 99/year. Subscription revenue AED 450K/month (annual/12 + monthly blend). LTV:CAC improves from 1.8:1 to 3.2:1. Phase 3 KSA funding case strengthened.

**Example 3 — KSA launch (M31):** Riyadh launch with 250 providers, Arabic app, Mada payment, anchor insurer. Month 1 Riyadh: 2,800 jobs. Combined platform: 39,500 jobs/month. UAE ops stable at 36,500/month — no degradation.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Sequential phase gates | Prevents over-expansion; capital efficient |
| MVP minimal scope | Fastest path to hypothesis validation |
| Phase 1 before subscription | Need volume and trust before retention product |
| UAE complete before KSA | Home market dominance reduces expansion risk |
| B2B pilot at M6 (not MVP) | B2C trust foundation required for insurer partnership |
| Integrated roadmap document | Single source of truth for investors and team |
| Optional GCC (Bahrain/Qatar) | Flexibility based on KSA performance |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Phase gate failure | High | Kill criteria; pivot playbook |
| Roadmap slip (common) | Medium | Buffer months; prioritize ruthlessly |
| Resource contention (UAE vs. KSA) | High | Dedicated KSA team; gate criteria |
| Feature creep across phases | Medium | Change control; phase scope documents |
| Market timing (competitor) | Medium | Speed on MVP; B2B lock-in |

## Assumptions

- Series A funding closes to support Phase 1–3 timeline
- Each phase completes within planned duration (±2 months acceptable)
- Team scaling keeps pace with geographic expansion
- No force majeure (pandemic-scale) disruption
- Roadmap reviewed and updated quarterly post-launch

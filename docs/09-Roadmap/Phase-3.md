## KSA Market Entry Strategy

### Why KSA

| Factor | Data |
|--------|------|
| Registered vehicles | ~12M (vs. UAE ~4.5M) |
| Roadside market estimate | 3.5× UAE SAM |
| Digital adoption | High mobile penetration; Vision 2030 digital push |
| Competition | Fragmented; no dominant digital marketplace |
| Distance premium | Higher AOV (longer tows; desert corridors) |

### KSA Launch Sequence

| City | Launch Month | Providers at Launch | Rationale |
|------|--------------|---------------------|-----------|
| **Riyadh** | M31 | 250 | Capital; largest vehicle population |
| **Jeddah** | M34 | 180 | Western region hub; Makkah corridor |
| **Dammam/Khobar** | M38 | 120 | Eastern province; logistics corridor |

### KSA Go-To-Market

| Channel | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| Provider acquisition | Field sales in industrial areas (Riyadh: Al Sulay, Jeddah: Kilo 14); zero-commission promo (20 jobs) |
| Customer acquisition | Performance marketing; highway QR; insurance B2B2C |
| B2B | Anchor insurer partnership (1 major Saudi insurer pre-launch) |
| Brand | Arabic-first; "Salik Assist" or localized brand TBD |
| Pricing | SAR equivalent of UAE guardrails; +15% AOV premium |

### KSA Volume Targets

| Month | KSA Jobs/Month | UAE Jobs/Month | Total |
|-------|----------------|----------------|-------|
| M33 (Riyadh +2 mo) | 3,500 | 36,000 | 39,500 |
| M36 | 8,000 | 38,000 | 46,000 |
| M42 | 15,000 | 40,000 | 55,000 |
| M48 | 25,000 | 40,000 | 65,000 |

## KSA Localization Requirements

### Legal & Compliance

| Requirement | Action | Timeline |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| KSA entity (LLC or branch) | Establish Riyadh entity | M28–M30 |
| SDAIA data compliance | Localize privacy program | M29–M31 |
| MCI e-commerce registration | Register platform | M30 |
| VAT (15% KSA) | TRN registration; pricing update | M31 |
| Transport authority permits | Verify provider licensing (TGA, etc.) | M29–M31 |
| Arabic terms & policies | Legal translation | M30 |

### Operational Localization

| Element | KSA Adaptation |
|---------|----------------|
| Language | Arabic-primary app and support |
| Payment | Mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay |
| Currency | SAR pricing and payouts |
| Support hours | AST (UTC+3); Ramadan adjusted |
| Service categories | All 6 + desert recovery (high demand) |
| Provider verification | KSA trade license, iqama, TGA permits |
| Highway network | Riyadh-Dammam (Route 40), Jeddah-Makkah (Route 5) |

### Cultural Considerations

- Female customer safety protocols enhanced (female provider option where available)
- Ramadan operating hours and surge adjustments
- Hajj/Umrah season demand planning (Jeddah/Makkah corridor)
- Desert recovery as core category (not niche)

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Riyadh launch:** Month 31. Motorist on King Fahd Road (Riyadh) has battery failure. Opens Salik Assist (Arabic interface). Three offers within 4 minutes. Accepts SAR 180 jump-start. Provider arrives in 22 minutes. Payment via Mada. First month: 2,800 Riyadh jobs.

**Example 2 — Cross-border insurer:** Month 40. Regional insurer with UAE and KSA operations signs unified contract. Policyholders in both countries access Salik through insurer app. 4,000 cross-border jobs/month by Month 45. Single account manager; unified SLA reporting.

**Example 3 — Desert recovery (KSA):** Month 35. Tourist vehicle stuck in desert outside Riyadh. Requests desert recovery through app. Specialized provider dispatched (45-minute ETA). Recovery + tow to garage: SAR 650. Premium category. 150 desert jobs/month in KSA by Month 42.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| KSA before smaller GCC states | 3.5× market size; strategic priority |
| Riyadh → Jeddah → Dammam sequence | Population and provider density logic |
| Separate KSA provider pool | Regulatory and operational clarity |
| Arabic-first in KSA | Market requirement; not optional |
| Optional Bahrain/Qatar in Phase 3 | Only if KSA successful; avoid over-extension |
| Regional GM structure | Local leadership required for KSA success |
| 250 KSA providers at Riyadh launch | Match UAE launch playbook scaled for city size |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| KSA regulatory barriers | High | Legal counsel; local entity; government relations |
| KSA provider recruitment slower | High | Early start (M29); zero-commission; competitive commission |
| Management attention split UAE/KSA | High | Dedicated KSA GM and team |
| Currency/revenue recognition complexity | Medium | Finance systems; SAR/AED reporting |
| Cultural missteps | Medium | Local hires; cultural training |
| Over-expansion to Bahrain/Qatar prematurely | Medium | Strict gate criteria |
| Funding insufficient for KSA | High | Series A KSA allocation (35% of raise) |

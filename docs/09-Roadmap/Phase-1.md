## Phase 1 Overview

**Duration:** Months 4–18 post-launch (~15 months)  
**Theme:** *Expand the marketplace — more services, more cities, more partners.*

Phase 1 transforms Salik Assist from a Dubai tow-and-battery MVP into a multi-category, multi-emirate marketplace with active B2B insurance and fleet channels.

| Dimension | MVP (End) | Phase 1 (End) |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Markets | Dubai | Dubai + Abu Dhabi + Sharjah |
| Service categories | 2 | 6 |
| Active providers | 350 | 900 |
| Monthly jobs | 7,000 | 22,000 |
| B2B job share | 0% | 18% |
| Net revenue (monthly) | AED 337K | AED 920K |

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Multi-category expansion:** Customer in Abu Dhabi (Month 11) runs out of fuel on E11. Selects fuel delivery — new Phase 1 category. Two offers in 3 minutes. Provider delivers 10L petrol. AED 95 charged. Customer rate 4 stars. Category contributes 5% of monthly volume but high acquisition value (unique service).

**Example 2 — Insurance B2B launch:** Month 7 insurance pilot. Policyholder taps "Roadside Help" in insurer app. Pre-authorized tow request. Provider completes job. Customer pays zero. Insurer billed AED 280. Platform earns AED 38.60 (12% + fee). 400 pilot jobs in Month 7 growing to 1,200 by Month 12.

**Example 3 — Provider subscription:** Month 8. Tow operator in Sharjah subscribes to Pro (AED 299/month). Matching boost yields 15 additional jobs/month at AED 40 contribution each = AED 600 incremental contribution. ROI positive; upgrades to Premium at Month 11.

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Abu Dhabi before Sharjah | Larger market; insurance HQs; higher AOV |
| B2B pilot Month 6 (not MVP) | MVP validates B2C; B2B requires stable ops |
| 6 categories by Month 10 | Breadth positioning vs. single-service competitors |
| Subscription tiers Month 6–8 | Recurring revenue; provider lock-in |
| 24/7 support by Month 9 | Required for Abu Dhabi and highway coverage |
| Phase 1 ends Month 18 | Allows 12 months post-MVP validation before Phase 2 investment |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Abu Dhabi provider recruitment slower than Dubai | High | Early field sales (M7); Dubai provider cross-coverage |
| Insurance partnership delays | High | Fleet channel as alternative B2B; extend pilot timeline |
| Category expansion dilutes quality | Medium | Stagger launches; category-specific training |
| Ops team scaling lag | Medium | Hire ahead of geographic launch by 2 months |
| Competitor launches in Abu Dhabi first | Medium | Speed to market; insurer exclusivity discussions |

## Customer Subscription Product

### Salik Assist Plus (Launch Month 19)

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Price** | AED 99/year or AED 12/month |
| **Includes** | 4 roadside events/year (any category); 20% discount on additional events; priority matching (+10% boost); free cancellation (2/year); dedicated support line |
| **Target segment** | Frequent drivers, families, expats, fleet-adjacent individuals |
| **Margin** | ~30% (assuming 1.2 events/subscriber/year at AED 35 platform cost/event) |
| **Adoption target** | 5% of registered users by Month 24; 12% by Month 30 |

### Subscription Business Case

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| LTV uplift per subscriber | +AED 60 over 24 months |
| Adjusted LTV:CAC (with subscription) | 2.5:1 → 3.5:1 at 12% adoption |
| Subscription revenue (Month 30) | AED 540K/month (45K subscribers × blended) |
| Churn target | <25% annual |

### Family Plan (Launch Month 24)

- AED 149/year; covers up to 4 vehicles
- Shared event pool (6 events/year)
- Target: 20% of Plus subscribers upgrade

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Subscription customer:** Expat family subscribes to Plus (AED 99/year) at Month 20. Uses 2 events in Year 1: battery jump (AED 0 out-of-pocket) and tow (AED 0). Third event gets 20% discount. Subscription NPS: 62. Refers 2 friends. LTV: AED 99 subscription + AED 45 referral value + AED 30 word-of-mouth = high-retention segment.

**Example 2 — EV roadside:** Month 23. Tesla owner stranded with depleted battery in Dubai Marina. Selects "EV Charging Rescue." Specialized provider dispatched with portable charger. 30-minute charge sufficient to reach nearest station. AED 350 charged. Premium margin category. 200 EV jobs/month by Month 30.

**Example 3 — Enterprise fleet:** Month 26. Logistics company with 200 vans on Salik fleet portal. 85 roadside events/month. Consolidated billing AED 22K/month. Platform revenue AED 3,500/month from this single client. Contract extended 2 years. Account manager QBR quarterly.

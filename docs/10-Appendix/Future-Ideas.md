## Idea Evaluation Criteria

Ideas evaluated on five dimensions before roadmap promotion:

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Strategic alignment** | 25% | Does it advance Vision 2031? |
| **Revenue potential** | 25% | Incremental net revenue at scale? |
| **Feasibility** | 20% | Can we execute with current/regional capabilities? |
| **Risk** | 15% | Regulatory, brand, operational risk acceptable? |
| **Timing** | 15% | Is the market ready? Do prerequisites exist? |

**Promotion threshold:** Weighted score ≥70/100 to advance to Phase 4 roadmap consideration.

## Business Examples

**Example 1 — Idea promotion (B2B3 Rental car):** Phase 2 evaluation. Local rental company (Budget UAE) interested in embedded roadside. Revenue potential: 500 jobs/month. Feasibility: high (B2B infrastructure exists). Score: 78/100. Promoted to Phase 4 H1 roadmap. 💡 → 🔍

**Example 2 — Idea parked (PC4 In-app SOS):** Evaluated H3. Strategic alignment moderate. Regulatory complexity high (emergency services classification). Risk score low. Parked until legal clarity. 💡 → ⏸️

**Example 3 — Idea rejected (REV8 Auction placement):** Evaluated H3. Revenue potential high but fairness risk critical — contradicts top-3 transparent offer model. Customer trust damage outweighs revenue. 💡 → ❌

## Business Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Separate Future Ideas from roadmap | Prevents scope creep; ideas captured but not committed |
| Horizon tagging (H1/H2/H3) | Matches Vision timeline; prevents premature execution |
| Evaluation criteria before promotion | Disciplined innovation; not every idea becomes a project |
| Business-facing ops/tech ideas only | Aligns with documentation scope (no architecture) |
| Quarterly review cadence | Ideas re-evaluated as market and platform evolve |
| Explicit rejection tracking | Prevents re-litigating rejected ideas without new evidence |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Idea backlog becomes distraction | Medium | Strict evaluation criteria; leadership gate |
| Competitor executes idea first | Medium | Monitor market; accelerate high-priority H1 ideas |
| Team frustration with parked ideas | Low | Transparent evaluation; revisit cadence |
| Regulatory-sensitive ideas pursued prematurely | High | Legal review before any H1 promotion |
| Data/advertising ideas erode trust | Medium | Customer trust prioritized in evaluation |

## Assumptions

- Platform achieves Phase 3 success (780K jobs/year) before most H2 ideas relevant
- Innovation backlog reviewed by leadership, not individual team members ad hoc
- Rejected ideas can be resurrected with new market evidence
- White-label licensing (B2B1) requires platform maturity not achieved until Year 3+
- Most H3 ideas align with Vision 2031 narrative, not near-term planning
